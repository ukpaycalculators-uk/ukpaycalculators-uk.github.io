Why write it down

Payslips, spreadsheets and online calculators often disagree. Usually it is by a few pounds, because of rounding or the way National Insurance is worked out each pay period. Sometimes it is by much more, and the cause is nearly always one of a short list: Scotland's six income tax bands, the personal allowance taper above £100,000, student loan thresholds, or the way a pension contribution is taken from pay.

Setting out each step, with every rate and threshold tied to the GOV.UK page it comes from, makes the figures checkable. If a number here is wrong, it can be traced to the step that produced it.

What these notes cover

  • Net pay, relief at source and salary sacrifice pension contributions

They do not cover self-employed profits, savings interest, benefits in kind or Self Assessment. For dividends there is a separate dividend tax calculator on the main site.
