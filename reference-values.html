If your figure is different

  • Tax code. Anything other than 1257L changes the allowance. A K code adds to taxable pay; BR, D0 and D1 tax all pay at one rate with no allowance.
  • Pay period. Payroll works out National Insurance per pay period. Uneven pay, such as a bonus or overtime in one month, gives a different yearly total from the even-pay figures here.
  • Pension. A net pay contribution lowers income tax only; salary sacrifice lowers tax, National Insurance and student loan repayments.
  • Scottish taxpayer status. It depends on where you live, not where you work.
  • Student loan plan. Thresholds range from £25,000 (Plan 5) to £33,795 (Plan 4), so the plan matters as much as the salary.
