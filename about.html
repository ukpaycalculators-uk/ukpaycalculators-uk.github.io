About these notes

UK Pay Notes is published by UK Pay Calculators, a free collection of UK pay and entitlement calculators run by Asquare AI Digital Labs Ltd.

Where the figures come from

Every rate and threshold is taken from GOV.UK, HMRC or the Scottish Government and checked against the published source before it is used. The values on these pages are produced by the same calculation engine as the calculators, so the two cannot drift apart. The full list of sources is on the main site's methodology page.

When they are updated

UK tax rates and thresholds change on 6 April each year, and some change at Budgets in between. These pages are updated for each new tax year, and the tax year is shown at the top and bottom of every page.

What they are not

These are general figures, for guidance. They are not financial advice and do not take account of anyone's personal circumstances.
